About Me

I'm a Solution Engineer and Full-Stack Developer based in Kigali, Rwanda, currently a Full-Stack Developer Fellow at Kosmotive through the AIMS-H2i Data Science Fellowship (Health Entrepreneurship, Africa Health Collaborative, University of Toronto). I hold an MSc in Mathematical Sciences (Data Science) from AIMS Rwanda and a BSc in Mathematics with Statistics from the University of Rwanda.

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, payments, secure systems and applied AI. I build and operate live platforms serving users across Rwanda, DRC, Central Africa and Kenya, covering REST integrations, MTN MoMo payments, database engineering, automated testing, CI/CD, security controls and audit trails. Before this I built production FinTech and climate analytics platforms, including KosmoInsight AI, AB Rwanda Risk Analytics and RASIP, and presented research at MLOA 2025 and SOSCHI 2025.
